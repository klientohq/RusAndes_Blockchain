The problem

A standalone website is not a commercial system.

Many businesses receive messages through WhatsApp, Instagram, email, and referrals but lack a consistent path from initial interest to follow-up.

01

The offer is not immediately clear

Visitors cannot quickly tell who the service is for, what it solves, or what to do next.

02

Potential customers remain scattered

Information reaches different channels and depends on memory or manual follow-up.

03

There is no foundation for improvement

Without useful events, Search Console, or qualified-lead criteria, decisions are made blindly.

How we work

Four stages. One clear scope.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    We review the offer, current channels, prospect journey, and definition of success.

  2. 02

    Build

    We design and develop the website around one offer and one primary action.

  3. 03

    Connect

    We integrate capture, measurement, and one agreed automation with error paths and controls.

  4. 04

    Measured handoff

    We test the complete path, document access, and leave a foundation for evidence-based improvement.

Good fit

Service businesses that need to organize lead capture.

  • Depend on WhatsApp, Instagram, referrals, or manual follow-up.
  • Have a valuable offer but a weak, outdated, or unmeasured website.
  • Can define a qualified lead and assign someone to respond.
  • Work with one responsible decision-maker who can provide information.
Boundaries

What we do not promise.

  • Guaranteed rankings, traffic, leads, sales, or revenue.
  • Custom enterprise software, 24/7 support, or unlimited revisions.
  • External messages or important decisions without agreed controls.
  • Integrations, case studies, or results that have not been demonstrated.
