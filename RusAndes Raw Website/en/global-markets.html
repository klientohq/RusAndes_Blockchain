Clear audience

A pathway for people who want to invest with a method.

Designed for professionals and independent earners who save or invest on their own and want to rely less on headlines, trends, and isolated recommendations.

It may fit you if…

  • You have assets or savings but no written policy.
  • You want to understand how asset classes work together.
  • You need to organize horizon, liquidity, and risk exposure.
  • You prefer an explainable process to impulsive decisions.

It is not a pathway for…

  • Signals, day trades, or return promises.
  • Personalized allocations, percentages, or assets.
  • Custody, management, or transfer of funds.
  • Individualized financial, legal, or tax advice.
The portfolio blueprint

Decisions come before assets.

The framework organizes the questions a person should address before investing. It does not produce a personalized recommendation.

Functions, not percentages.

A portfolio may combine layers with different jobs. Their presence and size are not universal.

Conceptual diagram. It does not represent a recommended allocation.

01Goal and constraints

We define what the capital is intended to fund and which conditions matter: near-term needs, currency, commitments, and personal limits.

02Time horizon and liquidity

We separate when money may be needed from how much should remain available. A long horizon does not remove short-term needs.

03Risk capacity and tolerance

We explain the difference between being able to absorb a loss and feeling comfortable with volatility. This is educational, not a regulatory suitability assessment.

04Roles and diversification

We compare function, risk, and concentration across asset classes, sectors, geographies, currencies, and issuers. Diversification cannot eliminate loss risk.

05Review rules

We organize questions for reviewing changes in goals, horizon, and exposure. We explain rebalancing without telling a person what to buy or sell.

06Cross-cutting costs and risks

We include costs, taxes, currency, volatility, counterparty exposure, custody, and information quality within the educational analysis.

Market universes we can explain

Understand the role and the risks, not only the asset name.

Frequently asked questions

Before requesting a session.

Will RusAndes build my portfolio for me?

No. We explain an educational framework. We do not provide a personalized allocation or tell clients what to buy, sell, or hold.

Do you recommend specific investments?

We do not provide individualized recommendations. Any asset or scenario shown is a general educational example only.

Does RusAndes receive or manage money?

No. RusAndes does not receive, custody, or manage funds, and does not execute or transmit orders.

Does diversification prevent losses?

No. It may reduce concentration risk, but it cannot guarantee gains or prevent losses.

Do I need prior experience?

No. We adjust the level of explanation while maintaining the same boundary: general education, not personalized recommendations.

What if I need a personalized recommendation?

You should consult a properly authorized firm or professional in your jurisdiction. The session can help you prepare better questions for that conversation.
