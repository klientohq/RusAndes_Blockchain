Frequently asked questions

Before you begin.

Does RusAndes guarantee admission or a scholarship?

No. RusAndes guides and organizes the process, but the final decision belongs to the university, scholarship program, and competent authorities.

Is RusAndes an official representative of these universities?

No. Institutions are listed because they publish international-admission pathways we can evaluate. RusAndes provides independent guidance and document organization; the candidate submits and controls the application through the official channel.

Does RusAndes have an office or team in all these cities?

No. Each destination is evaluated case by case, and inclusion does not imply a physical presence, partnership, or local team.

Are tuition prices shown in Colombian pesos?

No. They are shown in the currency published by the official source—primarily rubles—to avoid rapidly outdated conversions. Payment is made directly to the institution under its instructions.

Do I need to know Russian?

It depends on the program. There are English-taught options, Russian-taught options, and foundation faculties. Language and exam requirements must be confirmed for each program.

Does the service include a visa or legal immigration advice?

It may include general guidance and document organization, but not legal representation or a visa guarantee. When needed, consult the authority or a qualified professional.
